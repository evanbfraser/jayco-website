Jayco Companion App

Your Jayco,
in your pocket.

Ask any question and get an instant answer. Pull up your RV’s specs, manuals and nearby dealers in seconds. Work through pre-trip checklists, and find service information fast when something needs attention.

Free · iPhone and iPad · iOS 17 or later

Whether you are a first-timer or a seasoned traveler, the app is the co-pilot you didn’t know you needed: everything it takes to be a confident RV owner, from hitching up to hooking up utilities, in one place.

What the app does

  1. Ask

    Quickly search manuals, tips and FAQs with AI.

    Ask any question and get an instant AI-powered answer, drawn from Jayco’s own manuals, tips and FAQs — from hitching up to hooking up utilities.

  2. Your RV

    Your Jayco RV, at your fingertips.

    Its specs, owner’s manuals, videos and warranty, for the coach you own, in seconds. And pre-trip checklists, so nothing gets left behind.

  3. Service

    Maintenance and parts from certified dealers.

    When something needs attention, find service information and the nearest dealer fast.

  4. Community

    Stay connected to the Jayco community.

    A community feed from Jayco, with the RV show schedule in it, alongside everything else about your coach.

Get Jayco Companion.

Free on the App Store, for iPhone and iPad.
