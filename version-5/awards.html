About these awards.

An annual survey run by the RVDA — the Recreation Vehicle Dealer Association — in which dealers rate the manufacturers they carry on the things dealers care about: product quality, warranty handling, parts, and how the company behaves when something goes wrong. Jayco has won it for both towables and motorized every year on this page.

Because the award did not go to one. Reader's Choice awards and the Dealer Satisfaction Index are given to a brand or a whole lineup, so those rows say what category was won rather than naming a floorplan that did not win it.

Many of them, yes — the lineup carries forward year to year, though floorplans do come and go. The floorplan catalog is the current list, and Top-Selling RVs shows what people are actually buying now, which is a different question from what won a prize in 2022.

A cumulative total rather than a single year's award — 103 RVDA Dealer Satisfaction Index awards across the company's history. It does not belong to any one model year, which is why it is not in the list above.

An award is a good filter and a poor decision. Put two floorplans side by side in Compare, or start from how you travel with the RV Finder Quiz — the coach that fits your family and your tow vehicle beats the one with the better trophy.
