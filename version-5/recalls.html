Recalls

Check your VIN.

Recall notices are mailed to the owner Jayco has on file. You can also look up your own RV at any time, in the United States or in Canada.

Recall lookup

Where is your RV registered?

United States

  1. 1

    Find your VIN

    The 17-character number on the weight sticker. On a travel trailer or fifth wheel it is inside the entrance door and on the outside roadside wall; on a Class B, the driver’s doorjamb; on a Class C, inside the entrance door and the driver’s doorjamb; on a Class A, inside the entrance door and on the wall behind the driver’s seat.

  2. 2

    Look it up at NHTSA

    The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration’s lookup shows any open safety recall on your VIN.

    Check for recalls at NHTSA.gov (opens in a new tab)
  3. 3

    Book the repair

    If a recall is open, contact an authorized Jayco dealer to schedule it, with your VIN and the recall number to hand.

About recalls.

In the United States, enter your 17-character VIN at NHTSA’s recall lookup. In Canada, Jayco lists the recalls it has filed with Transport Canada since January 1, 2026 on the Canada tab, with each notice and owner’s letter.

By mail, to the owner Jayco has on file. If you bought your Jayco used or have moved, send the Change of Ownership Form so the letter reaches you.
