The Jayco Difference

We do things a little differently.

Since 1968, we have learned where better materials, smarter engineering and extra attention make the biggest difference.

The difference is not a package you add on. It is the material that went into the wall before the wall was closed up, the frame drawn around your floorplan rather than the other way round, and the warranty that stays with the structure after the first one runs out. That thinking shows up throughout the Jayco lineup — in the materials, engineering, technology, testing and support that go into every RV — and the point of all of it is the same: that the trips are safer, easier and longer lasting.

Four reasons to believe

  • 01

    Engineered Better

    In-house engineers test materials, construction methods and ride quality, and what they learn goes back into the build.

  • 02

    Built Better

    The parts you cannot see once the wall is closed — the roof, the frame, the laminate — are where the specification actually goes.

  • 03

    Inspected Better

    Every RV gets a dedicated pre-delivery inspection before it reaches the dealer, not a sample pulled off the line.

  • 04

    Backed Better

    A two-plus-three warranty, and a dealer and service network that is still there long after the paperwork is done.
