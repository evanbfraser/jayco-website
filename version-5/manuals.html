From 2025 onwards Jayco documents by class rather than by model: a travel trailer, a destination trailer, a fifth wheel and a toy hauler share their water, power, propane and running gear, so one book covers them. Before that there was a manual per model — Jay Flight, Eagle, Pinnacle and the rest — which is why the older years carry two dozen each. Every card lists what its manual covers.
