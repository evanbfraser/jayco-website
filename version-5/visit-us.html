Visit Us

Come and watch one being built.

Free factory tours in Middlebury, Indiana, two mornings a week. Pre-registration only, and worth the drive.

An hour on the floor tells you more about how a coach is put together than any amount of reading — you see the wall come out of the press, the frame before anything is on it, and the people doing the work. Afterwards there is an outdoor showroom with current models in it, which is the other half of the reason to come.

Tours are complimentary. You do have to book, and there are two of them: one that follows a fifth wheel through, one that follows a motorhome.

Location

Middlebury,
Indiana.

Twelve miles east of Elkhart, in the part of northern Indiana where most of the country's RVs are made. Jayco has been on this street since 1968.

Book a tour

Two routes,
two mornings.

Each tour follows one kind of coach through the plant, so they are genuinely different walks rather than the same walk on two days. Both start at noon Eastern.

Thursdays, 12:00 PM ET

The Fifth Wheel Tour

The towable line: frames, the vacuum-bonded wall press, the roof going on, and a coach coming off the end of it. If you are shopping fifth wheels, this is the one that will change how you look at them.

Register
Tuesdays, 12:00 PM ET

The Motorhome Tour

The motorized line, which starts with a chassis somebody else built and ends with a coach on top of it. You see the front cap, the belted seating going in, and the ride and handling work.

Register
  • One registration form covers a maximum of two people. If there are more of you, fill in another form — a group is not turned away, but it is not one booking either.
  • Closed shoes only. No flip-flops, sandals or open toes anywhere on the tour, and this is not negotiable at the door — it is a working factory floor.
  • Book ahead. Tours are pre-registration only; there is no walk-up list.
  • Hours can move. Call 574-825-0659 if you are travelling any distance to get here.
While you are here

The Visitors Center.

Two doors down from the plant at 903 S. Main Street, with the outdoor showroom alongside it — current models you can walk through without a salesperson attached.

Inside there is apparel and merchandise for the whole family of brands: Jayco, Entegra Coach, Starcraft, Open Range and Heartland.
