Towable roadside assistance

Help on the road, 24/7/365.

Every new Jayco travel trailer and fifth wheel with Goodyear® tires comes standard with the Tredit™ Advantage Program: roadside assistance powered by Urgent.ly, through a network of more than 45,000 service vehicles.

  • Towing
  • Tire changes
  • Lockout service
  • Jump-starts
  • Fuel delivery
  • Key replacement
